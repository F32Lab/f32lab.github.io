Legal

Privacy policy.

Last updated: 31 July 2026

1. Who is responsible for your data

F32Lab is operated by Fabio Oliveira de Sousa, a private individual based in Switzerland, acting as the data controller for the processing described here.

Address: Via Carlo Pasta 8b, 6850 Mendrisio, Ticino, Switzerland
Email: fabio.dso.2000[at]gmail.com

This policy explains what personal data is collected through this website and through the services F32Lab provides, why it is collected, and what rights you have over it. It is written to meet the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (revFADP) and, where it applies to you, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

2. What this site does not do

This website sets no cookies. It runs no analytics, no tracking pixels, no advertising scripts and no social media embeds. Fonts are served from this domain rather than from a third-party font service, so loading a page sends no data to any outside provider. There is no cookie banner because there is nothing to consent to.

3. Data you send through the contact form

When you submit the contact form, the following is collected:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • The service you are interested in
  • A reference website, if you provide one
  • Your desired frequency, if you provide one
  • The description of your request
  • The fact and time of your consent to this policy

Purpose: to evaluate your request, reply to you, and prepare a quote.
Legal basis: steps taken at your request prior to entering a contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)) and your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)); under the revFADP, processing that is necessary to respond to an enquiry you initiated.

Providing this data is voluntary, but without a name, email and description of your request there is no way to answer you.

Please do not send passwords or account credentials through the form. Where a service requires access to a protected portal, those credentials are collected separately and only after a quote has been confirmed.

4. Data collected automatically

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub records technical connection data when a page is requested, including your IP address, the time of the request, the page requested and your browser's user agent string. This happens at the hosting layer and F32Lab does not have access to these logs or build any profile from them.

Legal basis: legitimate interest in operating and securing the website (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).

6. Transfers outside Switzerland and the EU

The providers listed above process data in the United States. Such transfers take place on the basis of the Swiss–US and EU–US Data Privacy Framework and/or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, together with the safeguards set out in each provider's data processing agreement. A copy of the relevant safeguards can be requested at the address in section 1.

7. How long data is kept

  • Enquiries that do not lead to a contract: deleted no later than 12 months after the last contact.
  • Enquiries that lead to a contract: kept for the duration of the service and afterwards as required by law.
  • Accounting records: retained for 10 years, as required by Art. 958f of the Swiss Code of Obligations.
  • Access credentials supplied for a service: deleted immediately when the service ends or when you ask, whichever is sooner.

8. Security

The site is served exclusively over HTTPS. Any credentials entrusted for a service are stored encrypted, used only for the agreed automation, and never shared. Access is limited to the controller named in section 1. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but the measures in place are appropriate to the sensitivity of the data involved.

9. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Ask what personal data is held about you and receive a copy
  • Have inaccurate data corrected
  • Have your data deleted
  • Restrict or object to processing
  • Receive your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing already carried out

To exercise any of these, write to the email address in section 1. Requests are answered within 30 days and are free of charge.

If you believe your data has been handled unlawfully, you may lodge a complaint with the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC), edoeb.admin.ch. If you are in the EU, you may instead complain to the supervisory authority where you live — in Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, garanteprivacy.it.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the services change or the law does. The current version is always the one published on this page, with the date of the last revision shown at the top.
